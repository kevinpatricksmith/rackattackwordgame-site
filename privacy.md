# Privacy Policy

**Rack Attack Word Game** (the app)

Last updated: 9 October 2026

## The short version

The developer collects no data from you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking
and no third-party code in the app. What you do in the game stays on your device, and, if you play a friend,
in your own Game Center account.

## What the app stores, and where

- **On your device only.** Your nickname, your avatar (one of the built-in pictures, or a photo you choose),
  your settings, your games in progress, and the history and statistics of the games you have played. They
  are kept in the app's own storage on your device. The developer cannot see them, and they are never sent to
  the developer.
- **Your photo.** If you choose a photo as your avatar, the app reads only the one photo you pick, crops it to
  a square, shrinks it, and keeps the small copy on your device. The app never asks for access to your photo
  library and cannot browse it. The photo is never uploaded.
- **Game Center, only if you play a friend.** A match against a friend uses Apple's Game Center. The match data
  (the board, the moves and the tiles needed to continue the game) is stored by Apple in the Game Center
  accounts of the two players, and the other player sees your Game Center name. Your custom nickname and
  your photo are not shared; both players see Game Center names. How Apple handles Game Center data is
  described in [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Playing against the computer
  or passing the device to someone beside you uses no Game Center and sends nothing anywhere.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect personal information, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers for the developer.
- It does not contain advertising, analytics or any third-party software development kit.
- It does not track you, and does not share data with data brokers or other companies.
- It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data.
- It makes no network connections of its own. The only network use is Apple's Game Center when you choose to
  play a friend.

## Children

The app has no accounts, no chat, no purchases and no links that leave the app other than the policy and
support pages. It collects nothing from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your device. Delete a game from the "In progress" list on the home screen, or
delete the app, and it is removed. Match data held in Game Center is managed through Game Center and your Apple
account.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
